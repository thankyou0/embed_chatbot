B. Crawl Progress Percentage

The core challenge: you don't know total pages upfront. Here are approaches:

Sitemap-based estimation: When sitemap is available, count total URLs in sitemap → use as total. Show crawled / sitemap_total (e.g., "45/312 pages (14%)")

Discovery-based estimation: Track two numbers — pages_crawled and urls_discovered (URLs found but not yet crawled, i.e., the queue size). Show progress as crawled / (crawled + queue_remaining) — this gives a dynamic % that starts low and converges to 100%

Historical estimation: For re-crawls, use the previous crawl's page count as the expected total

Time estimation: Track crawl speed (pages/min) and show "~X minutes remaining" based on queue size. Formula: estimated_time = urls_in_queue / crawl_speed_per_minute
